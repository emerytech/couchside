Manage Decky Loader from your phone. Install, repair, update or remove Decky Loader on your box, browse and install plugins from the official Decky store, and see what's installed — all from Setup, without leaving the couch. (Turn it on once on the box; the app tells you how.)

New too:
- Skins — give the Console a whole new shape in Setup → Appearance. Six looks, from the quiet "Studio" to the machined "Panel", each shown as a live preview and layered on top of your colour pack and accent.
- An optional alarm pulse that flags a hot vital from the corner of your eye.

Coming from an older version? You also get theme packs (OLED Black, Steam, Nord, Dracula and more), a calmer Console that opens to what's happening now, "Did you know" tips, card reorder on every tab, and an auto-drop option so the phone can't take player one mid-game.

Plus polish and reliability fixes — including a secure connection that heals itself after a box update, so the app no longer sits reconnecting until you reboot.
