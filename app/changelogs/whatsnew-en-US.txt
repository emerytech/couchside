Make the app your own. The customize gesture that was on the Console tab now works on the Fleet and Actions tabs too: tap the sliders icon in the corner (or press and hold any card) to reorder cards and hide the ones you don't use. Your layout is remembered.

Coming from an older version? This update also brings:
- Theme packs — give the whole app a new look: OLED Black (true black for OLED screens), Steam, Nord, Dracula, Solarized, High Contrast and more, in Setup → Appearance.
- A calmer Console that opens to what's happening right now, with lights, display and tools tucked behind one "More" tap.
- "Did you know" tips that gently point out features you might have missed — one at a time, never naggy.
- Auto-drop the pad: an option so that, while a game is running, your phone stops acting as a controller and can't take player one from your real gamepad.

Plus polish and reliability fixes throughout.
