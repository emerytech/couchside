Theme packs — give the whole app a new look. Choose OLED Black (true black, made for OLED screens), Steam, Nord, Dracula, Solarized, High Contrast and more in Setup → Appearance. The accent picker still layers on top.

A calmer Console — it opens to what's happening right now (media, streaming, a running game, your box's vitals), with lights, display, audio and tools tucked behind one "More" tap. A new button in the header lets you reorder or hide any card — tap it, make the Console yours.

Auto-drop the pad — for playing with a real controller: turn it on and, while a Steam game is running, your phone stops presenting a controller so it can't take player one from your gamepad. The full pad comes back the moment the game exits.

"Did you know" tips — occasional one-line hints introduce features you might have missed, right where they live. One at a time, at most once a day, never naggy — and you can turn them off with the onboarding tour.

Plus polish and reliability fixes throughout.
