Wake your gaming box directly from your iPhone.

Couchside now uses Apple's approved local-network multicast capability to send Wake-on-LAN packets immediately, before trying another box as a relay. Wake progress and connection confirmation remain visible, and relay fallback is still available.

Wake support depends on your network and your gaming box's hardware settings. Allow Local Network access when prompted.
