Build your own light shows. If your box has an addressable RGB strip (like a Steam Machine's), the Light card now has a frame-by-frame editor: paint each frame, set how long it holds, and the strip loops your animation — running on the box, so it keeps going with the app closed and survives a reboot. Two new built-in shows to start with: Rainbow River and Heartbeat. Pairing is quicker too — entering the sixth PIN digit connects automatically. Plus reliability fixes and polish.
