Build your own light shows. If your box has an addressable RGB strip (like a Steam Machine's), the Light card gains a frame-by-frame editor: paint each frame, set its timing, and the strip loops your animation — on the box, so it runs with the app closed and survives a reboot. Two new presets to start: Rainbow River and Heartbeat. Pairing is quicker too — the sixth PIN digit connects automatically. Plus fixes and polish.
